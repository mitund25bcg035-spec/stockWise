## Brand & Style

This design system blends Swiss-inspired information architecture with high-utility enterprise SaaS ergonomics. Designed for multi-channel logistics operators, inventory analysts, and supply-chain directors, it prioritizes legibility, dense metric presentation, and low-latency interaction.

### Visual Aesthetic & Philosophy
- **Editorial Precision:** Clean hairline rules, proportional spacing, and typographic weight contrast replace decorative chrome. Layouts mimic bespoke financial broadsheets and technical ledgers.
- **Controlled Contrast:** A high-end dark structural frame anchors the interface, offset by paper-toned work surfaces that reduce optic fatigue during extended operational monitoring.
- **Targeted Chromatic Accents:** Chromatic energy is reserved strictly for functional feedback—metrics, alerts, status flags, and primary triggers—never purely decorative washes.
- **Ergonomic Tactility:** Subtle transitions (120ms–150ms cubic-bezier), hairline borders, and shallow spatial lifts deliver responsiveness without distracting from data comprehension.

## Colors

The palette balances authoritative deep forest tones with crisp, luminescent signals designed for instant scanning across complex data grids.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary / Structural Anchor (`#14211D`):** Applied to persistent navigation, high-level headers, and key interactive commands. Communicates permanence and executive control.
- **Primary Accent / Action (`#C7E78B`):** An electric, acid-tinged lime reserved for primary CTA states, active sidebar markers, micro-progress bars, and positive delta values. It ensures critical calls-to-action immediately command attention.
- **System Signals & Indicators:**
  - **Healthy / Inflow (`#B6D8C9`):** Soft electric sage denoting incoming stock, operational efficiency, and safe thresholds.
  - **Critical Alert / Depletion (`#E9A18D`):** Warm coral flagging low-stock warnings, system anomalies, and destructive triggers.
  - **Categorical & Analytical Segments (`#C9C2E6`):** Muted lavender allocated to secondary chart vectors, batch breakdowns, and structural metadata tags.
- **Neutral Canvas Hierarchy:**
  - **Base Canvas (`#F5F7F3`):** Neutral paper ground minimizing glare across long operational shifts.
  - **Surface Container (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity white for data cards, modal dialogues, and tabular surfaces.
  - **Structural Division (`#E2E8E4`):** Crisp 1px boundary rules for borders, table rows, and grid axes.
  - **Typography Ink (`#17211E` Primary / `#7C8982` Secondary):** High-contrast contrast levels meeting strict WCAG AAA guidelines for text-dense environments.

## Typography

The typography pairs **Manrope** for authoritative structural framing and quantitative displays with **DM Sans** for sustained tabular and analytical reading.

### Rules & Application
- **Tabular Numerics:** Enable `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums lining-nums` across all `Manrope` instances within data tables, KPI displays, and ledger rows to guarantee horizontal alignment.
- **Metric Presentation:** Use `numeric-metric` for top-level inventory summaries. Pair with an adjacent `label-sm` rendered in uppercase for categorical clarity.
- **Density Management:** For screen viewports under 768px, swap `headline-lg` with `headline-lg-mobile` to maintain viewport economy and avoid unwieldy string wrapping.
- **Body Rhythm:** Never set line length wider than 65 characters in operational notes or logs. Default table content uses `body-sm` to maintain vertical compactness without sacrificing baseline rhythm.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial architecture employs an 8-point baseline rhythm combined with a Swiss-inspired fluid 12-column grid.

### Layout System
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column layout with a fixed 260px primary navigation rail. Grid columns use `gutter-desktop` (1.5rem / 24px) with canvas margins of `margin-desktop` (2rem / 32px).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout. The rail collapses to an 80px icon navigation bar. Gutter drops to `gutter` (1rem / 16px), and margins compress to `margin` (1rem / 16px).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column single-stream stack with drawer-anchored navigation. Edge margins locked to `margin` (1rem / 16px).

### Spacing Principles
- **Interior Component Rhythm:** Table cells use `space-sm` vertically and `space-md` horizontally. Metric card interiors maintain a strict `space-lg` padding to isolate figures visually.
- **Section Breaks:** Major analytical card blocks separate by `space-xl`. Grouped ledger filters and toolbars use `space-sm` gaps for unified control groupings.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows, relying instead on structural layering, tonal surfaces, and low-contrast perimeter rules to create hierarchy.

### Depth Architecture
- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** Surface tint `#F5F7F3`. No shadow, completely non-interactive ground.
- **Layer 1 (Cards, Metric Containers, Grids):** Surface `#FFFFFF` bordered by a 1px continuous rule `#E2E8E4`. Supported by an ultra-diffused shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(20, 33, 29, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(20, 33, 29, 0.02)`.
- **Layer 2 (Dropdowns, Popovers, Floating Action Trays):** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E2E8E4`, elevated via: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(20, 33, 29, 0.06), 0 10px 24px -2px rgba(20, 33, 29, 0.08)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals, Overlays):** Surface `#FFFFFF`, boundary border 1px solid `#E2E8E4`, cast shadow: `0 20px 35px -4px rgba(20, 33, 29, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(20, 33, 29, 0.04)`. Accompanied by a `#14211D` backdrop overlay at 60% opacity with a `2px` gaussian backdrop filter.
- **Active Navigation Elements:** Structural sidebar items sit on `#14211D` without ambient elevation; selected items are carved out via `#C7E78B` left-edge tabs (3px) and high-contrast text shifts.

## Shapes

The geometric framework balances technical rigor with modern ergonomics through calibrated corner radii.

### Shape Tokens & Radius Scale
- **Base Surfaces (`rounded-lg` / 1rem):** All metric cards, data visualization containers, slide-out panels, and data tables use 1rem (16px) corners.
- **Controls & Form Elements (`rounded` / 0.5rem):** Standard input fields, search wrappers, filter dropdowns, and button containers use 0.5rem (8px) corners to preserve a crisp footprint within dense layouts.
- **Tags, Pills, & Badges (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Operational tags (e.g., SKU category, batch status, inventory movement indicators) use fully rounded pill shapes to distinguish them from actionable buttons and cards.
- **Icon Buttons:** Small interactive iconography (18–20px Lucide icons) sits within a 36x36px bounding container with 0.5rem (8px) rounding.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#C7E78B`, text `#14211D`, typography `label-lg`. No border. Hover state transitions to `#B8DC7A`. Active scale down to `0.98`. Focus ring: 2px solid `#14211D` with a 2px offset.
- **Secondary / Technical Action:** Background `#14211D`, text `#FFFFFF`, typography `label-lg`. Hover brings background to `#23332D`.
- **Tertiary / Outlined:** Background `transparent`, border 1px solid `#E2E8E4`, text `#17211E`. Hover triggers background `#F5F7F3`.

### Chips & Status Badges
- **General Rules:** Height locked to 24px, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `0 10px`, typography `label-sm` (uppercase).
- **In-Stock / Healthy:** Background `#B6D8C9` at 30% alpha, text `#0E4331`, indicator dot 6px solid `#28815F`.
- **Low Stock / Reorder Warning:** Background `#E9A18D` at 25% alpha, text `#732B1A`, indicator dot 6px solid `#C24C2C`.
- **Category Pill:** Background `#C9C2E6` at 30% alpha, text `#362C5C`.

### Cards & Ledger Blocks
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E2E8E4`, `rounded-lg` (16px), interior padding `space-lg` (24px).
- **Header Structure:** Flex layout with `space-between` alignment; title rendered in `headline-sm` with action menus pinned right.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text & Search Inputs:** Height 40px, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E2E8E4`, radius `rounded` (8px), padding `0 12px`, typography `body-md`. Focus state replaces border with `#14211D` and a 1px outer ring in `#14211D`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 18x18px bounding box, border 1.5px solid `#7C8982`, radius 4px (checkbox) or circular (radio). Checked state fills `#14211D` with `#C7E78B` checkmark/indicator.

### Data Tables & Lists
- **Header Rows:** Background `#F5F7F3`, typography `label-md` in uppercase, height 40px, bottom border 1px solid `#E2E8E4`.
- **Data Rows:** Background `#FFFFFF`, height 48px, horizontal cell padding `space-md`, border-bottom 1px solid `#E2E8E4`. Hover row state: `#F9FAF7`. Monospaced tabular numerals enabled.

### Inventory-Specific: Metric Gauges & Micro Charts
- **Bar / Progress Metrics:** Track height 6px, radius 3px, track background `#E2E8E4`. Fill uses `#C7E78B` (normal levels) transitioning to `#E9A18D` below min threshold.
- **Sparklines:** Hairline 1.5px stroke width. Primary vector colored in `#14211D` with subtle gradient falloff into `#FFFFFF` at zero baseline.
